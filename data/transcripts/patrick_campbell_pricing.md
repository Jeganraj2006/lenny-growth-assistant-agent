Title: Patrick Campbell on Pricing Frameworks and Value Metrics
Guest: Patrick Campbell
URL: https://www.lenny-podcast.com/episodes/patrick-campbell-pricing

Most SaaS companies underprice their product by 3x because they price on feature lists rather than value metrics. A value metric is the unit by which your customers measure value from your product—such as seats, active users, contacts, or processed revenue. When you align your pricing tier upgrades with your customer's value metric, expansion revenue happens automatically without sales friction.
